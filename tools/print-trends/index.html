Before you sell a print

Sellable for free: CC BY, CC BY-SA and CC0 licences — credit the designer in your listing.

Not sellable: anything marked NC (non-commercial), "personal use", or Cults "PU", and any fan-art of brands or characters, whatever its licence says.

Paid route: many designers sell a commercial licence or a "merchant" tier on Patreon, Cults (CU licence) or MakerWorld. Buy that before listing.
